OUR STORY

From a Dream to Nsukka's Most Loved Hotel.

Lumina started with a simple idea: Nsukka deserved a hotel that matches international standards without losing our Igbo warmth.

What began as a 20-room boutique lodge in 2018 has grown into a 5-star destination for business travelers, couples, and families. Every corner, from our scent to our bedsheets, was chosen to make you feel at home, but better.

"We don't just give you a key, we give you peace of mind." — Founder, Lumina Hotel & Suites
